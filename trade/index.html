How values work and sources

Value is how many points a player adds over a replacement you could grab off waivers, summed over the games left, then stretched so stars are worth far more than depth. In Superflex one starting spot can be a QB, so the replacement QB is a starter-level QB instead of a free pickup, and good QBs jump up. The size of that jump (about 1.4x) is our judgment call to match the written advice below. No 2026 source publishes an exact Superflex number.
