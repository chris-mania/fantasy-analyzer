Add Fantasy Mania to your home screen

This is not an App Store app. It is this website, saved to your phone like a shortcut. Done the right way it opens full screen with no search bar or browser buttons, so it feels just like a real app. It is free, it updates by itself, and takes about 20 seconds.

1Tap the Share button

Open this site in Safari on your iPhone. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (the square with an arrow pointing up).

2Choose Add to Home Screen

Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen. If you do not see it, tap View More at the bottom of the list.

3Keep Open as Web App on, then tap Add

Leave the Open as Web App switch turned on. That is what hides the browser search bar. Then tap Add in the top right.

Do not use a private tab. Your saved team and league live on your device, and private mode forgets them. Open the site in a normal Safari tab first, then add it to your Home Screen.

Tip: use Safari on iPhone or iPad. On Android, open the site in Chrome, tap the three dots menu, then Add to Home screen (or Install app).

How Mania works
Mania RatingHow strong his role and production have been this season, compared with other players at his position. It blends production, volume, team role, red-zone work and efficiency.
Week RatingHis Mania Rating adjusted for this week: the opponent, how players with a similar role have done against that defense, and his recent usage.
Percentiles and colorsEvery bar shows where he ranks among players at his position. Green is the top 30%, yellow is the middle, red is the bottom 40%.
